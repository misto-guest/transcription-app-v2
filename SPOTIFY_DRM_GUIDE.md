For Spotify episodes with DRM protection, you need to record the audio first, then transcribe.

## Step 1: Record Spotify Episode with NoteBurner

### What is NoteBurner?
A Spotify recorder that captures DRM-protected audio and saves it as MP3.
- **Download**: https://www.noteburner.com/spotify-music-converter/
- **Free trial available** (check current limits)
- **Works on macOS, Windows**

### Recording Instructions

1. **Install NoteBurner** on your Mac
2. **Open NoteBurner** and Spotify will launch
3. **Play the episode** you want to transcribe
4. **NoteBurner will automatically record** to MP3
5. **Stop recording** when episode ends
6. **Find the MP3 file** in NoteBurner's output folder

**Tip:** Set output folder to an easy location (e.g., `~/Downloads/Spotify`)
